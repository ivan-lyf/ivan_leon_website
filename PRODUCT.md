# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring Leon Meng’s personal work and interests through a room and its interactive computer. The shared repository also serves Ivan Luo’s separate classic Macintosh scene.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio experienced as a sparse penthouse above a rainy, retro-futuristic city. Visitors can look around the room, observe the city and enter the computer to read portfolio content.

## Brand Personality

Atmospheric, tactile, personal. The established references are the realism and interactions of Felix Rieseberg’s room, Blade Runner’s cinematic city atmosphere, and late-1980s/1990s electronic signage and computers.

## Anti-references

Glossy generic futuristic interfaces, excessive glitch noise, indiscriminate neon bloom, cluttered rooms, and advertising that overwhelms the architecture. Keep company identities distinct from tenant banners.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the sparse room and its authored composition.
- Make details rewarding to discover, but keep primary interactions and city accents visible from the starting view.
- Use restrained motion, cinematic light and material depth rather than excessive decoration.
- Maintain equivalent composition across screen sizes and constrain rendering cost on laptops.
- Preserve Ivan’s independent scene and the shared desktop engine.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing experience supports keyboard navigation, reduced motion, a motion pause control, and an accessible desktop fallback. New loading UI must expose genuine progress, provide a usable failure route and avoid adding artificial wait time.
