The chosen photograph was visually inspected from the project’s existing working reference. Its towers have substantial opaque cladding, deep mechanical floors, narrow openings, asymmetrical piers, rooftop plant, and different construction eras. This revision applies those observations to the existing seeded city coordinates. It introduces deep physical retrofit skins, service shafts, patched panels, conduit pairs, structural braces, docking decks, interrupted maintenance lights, and roof plant. It does not reproduce an individual film building or distribute the reference pixels.

The previous continuous decorative orbital rings and advertising spectacle are handled separately by the main city revision. The architecture’s emissives are small embedded maintenance and docking markers.
