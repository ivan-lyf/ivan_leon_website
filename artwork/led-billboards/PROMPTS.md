## KAO — main illustrated source

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original flat artwork texture for an animated vertical outdoor LED billboard in Blender. Generate ONE tightly cropped stylized Japanese adult female face, mysterious and poised, inspired by kabuki red eye makeup, ukiyo-e portrait composition and late-1980s/1990s anime title illustration. Portrait aspect 2:3. Face fills almost entire frame, cropped forehead and lower chin, black hair merging into pure deep black background, elongated dark almond eyes, dramatic angular burnt-red makeup sweeping around the eyes, restrained closed mouth and graphic nose. Asymmetrical hard planar facial shadows. Restricted palette ONLY deep black, electric cyan, hot magenta, ultraviolet purple and burnt red-orange with occasional pale cyan highlights. AUTHENTIC pixel-art electronic advertising graphic, NOT photography or glossy render: image composed of clearly legible chunky pixel clusters, stepped diagonal contours, stippled ordered halftone dithering, crisp hard color regions, fragmented thin horizontal scanlines. Keep large face silhouette and expression exceptionally clear while a few cheek and hair regions dissolve into coarse colored pixels. Flat graphic billboard art occupying the full image, NO actual screen, building, perspective, frame, labels, lettering or watermark. No smooth gradients, no realistic skin texture, no photographic lighting, no holographic UI, no bloom haze. Sparse localized horizontal row misalignment, otherwise stable composition. This is the base art; LED emitters and animation will be added physically in Blender.
